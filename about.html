About

I've been interested in the costs of bringing back the bison since before it became official. When a bison mascot showed up at last year's little 500, I was curious if it was a "soft launch" of the new mascot. The official introduction of the bison at the start of the first football game was a surprise, but not unexpected given the team's success the year before. It made sense to me that IU would be looking for a way to take advantage of the increased attention to IU sports.

What shocked me the most was that the bison had been in the works since 2023, before Cignetti arrived and before the football team got good.

The information for this project was sourced from public records and contracts acessed through APRA requests. This is likely not a comprehensive list, as there are contracts and invoices that may have been missed by me or the General Counsel.

All assets are original and code was sourced from class snippets or examples online, which are credited within the code and in my process log.
